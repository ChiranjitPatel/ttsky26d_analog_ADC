# material/

Reference images and explanatory material for reading the design: drawings, diagrams, notes.
These are **not** the source of truth. Circuits live in `xschem/`, simulation results and their
plots in `sim/<block>/`.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `schematics/` | Simple transistor-level drawings of each cell (generated by `draw_stages.py`, schemdraw): stage 1 bias_gen, stage 2 ota, stage 3 bandgap_core, stage 4 ptat_out, stage 5 VREF chain, stage 6 comparator, stage 7 inmux + vcm_gen, stage 8 cap_dac |

Add new subfolders by topic (e.g. `diagrams/`, `layout/`, `notes/`) and list them here.
