# Layout brief — chunk 1b: `ota` (bandgap error amplifier)

**Status: draft 2026-11-08 (night), sizes from the bandgap Phase 2 Monte Carlo.** Final once the bandgap
corner sweep is in (`sim/bandgap/corners.md`).
Schematic: `xschem/ota.sch` (generated by `xschem/gen_ota.py`). Netlist: `sim/out/netlist/ota.spice`.
Why these sizes: `sim/bandgap/README.md` ("Phase 2 — mismatch"). The OTA offset is multiplied by about 10 onto VBG,
so the input pair and the loads are large on purpose. **Matching is the main job of this layout.**

## What it does

This is a two-stage Miller OTA with a PMOS-lvt input. Inputs `inp` = `vb` and `inn` = `va` come from the bandgap
core (about 0.6–0.7 V), and the output `out` = `vgp` drives the gates of the bandgap PMOS current sources. It is
biased from `bias_gen` (`pbias`, 1 µA per pfet_lvt 2/2 unit). Power-down is `pd` = 1.

## Matching (CLAUDE.md rule 6)

1. **Input pair M1/M2:** split each into **2 × 16/8** (LVS merges parallel devices) and interdigitate as
   **`D  A B B A  D`** (common centroid, A = M1, B = M2). Dummies are pfet_01v8_lvt 16/8 on VDPWR. Use one
   n-well and the same orientation, with an n-tap guard ring around the pair. Keep `tail` short and
   symmetric to both halves.
2. **Loads M3/M4 + M6:** one row of 4/16 units: **`D  M6 M6 M3 M4 M6 M6  D`**. M3 and M4 sit in the middle,
   next to each other, and the M6 units are symmetric around them. Dummies are nfet 4/16 on VGND. Put a p-tap
   guard ring around the row.
3. **Tail M5 and load M7** are 2/2 units from `pbias`, like the bias_gen unit devices. Their matching isn't
   critical.
4. **CC over the devices:** the 32 × 32 µm MIM (met3 bottom plate `cz`, met4 top plate `out`) can sit over the
   input pair or the load row. Then no met3 routing is allowed there. Put `out` (top plate, met4) on the
   low-impedance side. That's already the schematic's orientation (c1 = out).
5. Keep `inp`/`inn` (to the bandgap's `vb`/`va`) symmetric, short, and away from `out`/`vgp`.

## Outline and pins

This sits inside `bandgap_core` (54 × 94 µm in the floorplan), next to `pnp_array` and `bg_res`
(`layout_briefs/scripted_cells.md`). A target outline of about 40 × 40 µm with CC on top works. Pins:
`inp`, `inn`, `out`, `pbias`, `pd`, VDPWR, VGND on met1/met2, with labels named exactly like that.
