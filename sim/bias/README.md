# Stage 1 — bias_gen (1 µA bias current reference)

Schematic: `xschem/bias_gen.sch` (generated by `xschem/gen_bias_gen.py`; edit sizes there and re-run).
Run: `../../tools/netlist.sh bias_gen && ngspice -b tb_bias.spice > ../out/bias/tb_bias.log && python3 report.py`

## Design

β-multiplier: MN1 (nfet_01v8 2/2, diode) and MN2 (4 × 2/2, source degenerated by R1 =
res_xhigh_po_0p35, L = 11 µm ≈ 63 kΩ); PMOS mirror MP1/MP2 = pfet_01v8_lvt 2/2. The low-Vt PMOS
saves ≈ 0.55 V of headroom compared with pfet_01v8 at the same current (stage 0 LUT).

**Interface for other blocks:** a `nfet_01v8 W=2 L=2` with gate on `nbias` sinks 1 µA; a
`pfet_01v8_lvt W=2 L=2` with gate on `pbias` sources 1 µA. Use multiples (`mult`) of these unit
devices, with the same L, for other currents.

Startup: when no current flows, MS2 (weak PMOS, 0.42/50, drawn as 5 × 0.42/10 in series MS2A–E so it fits
the layout outline) pulls `su` high, MS1 pulls `pbias` low,
and current starts. Once running, MS3 (a copy of the bias current) pulls `su` to ≈ 13 mV, so MS1 is off.
Power-down (`pd` = 1) forces `pbias` high and `nbias`/`su` low, and cuts the MS2 current.

## Phase 1 results (tt, 27 °C, 1.8 V)

See `results.md` (generated). All Phase 1 targets met: 1.004 µA sink, 0.967 µA source, 2.15 µA
own consumption, 0.7 nA when powered down, starts from 0 V on both a 1 µs and a 100 µs supply ramp.

## Notes and open points for Phase 2

1. **Current is PTAT-like and resistor-dependent.** I ≈ n·VT·ln4 / R1, and xhigh_po has TC1 ≈ −1470 ppm/K,
   so expect the bias current to rise strongly with temperature and to vary with the resistor corner.
   That's fine for biasing (roughly constant gm), but check it in Phase 2 and give consumers margin.
2. **Source vs sink mismatch (3.6 %)** comes from the different Vds of MP1/MP2 and the output device.
   It's acceptable for biasing; cascodes aren't needed at this accuracy.
3. **`su` dips to ≈ −0.38 V during the supply ramp** (capacitive coupling through MS1 before the core
   turns on). It's clamped by the junctions and startup still works, but re-check it at ss/−40 °C with slow ramps.
4. Line regulation is 6.4 % over 1.62–1.98 V (target < 10 %), without cascodes.

## Changes for layout (chunk 1a, 2026-11-08)

- **MS2 split into 5 × 0.42/10 in series** (MS2A–E). A 50 µm gate doesn't fit the 22 × 26 µm outline.
  Function unchanged: fast-ramp startup settles 0.12 µs after the ramp (was 0.06 µs), slow-ramp
  startup at VDPWR = 0.95 V (was 0.94 V), the running `su` is 13 mV. The `su` dip during the ramp is
  smaller (−0.14 V instead of −0.38 V).
- **`tb_bias.spice` uses `.options method=gear`.** With trapezoidal integration, after the 1 µs startup ramp the
  unit sink showed 0.90 µA for tens of µs, while every node voltage was already at its DC value
  (a numerical error current, gone with gear or with reltol 1e-5; same check with the old netlist: 1.004 µA).

## Phase 2 (2026-11-08)

`../../tools/run_corners.py tb_bias.spice --corners tt,ff,ss,fs,sf --temps=-40,27,125 --vdd 1.62,1.8,1.98`
then `python3 corners_report.py` → `corners.md`, `plots/corners.png`. All 45 runs pass (summary in `corners.md`).
Changes it caused:
- **Power-down switches and the pd inverter are no longer minimum length** (switches L = 0.5, inverter
  L = 1). At L = 0.15 the inverter PMOS leaked 1.5 µA at 125 °C in power-down; now the worst case is 43 nA.
- **The testbench drives `pd` from VDPWR** (`EPD`, gain 0/1), not a fixed 1.8 V source. At 1.98 V a 1.8 V
  "high" left the PMOS switches partly on.
- **Nodesets for `.op`.** At tt/27 °C/1.98 V the plain `.op` converged to a non-physical point. The report
  cross-checks each `.op` current against the DC sweep.
- **Layout dummies** MDN1/2 and MDP1/2 are in the schematic.
