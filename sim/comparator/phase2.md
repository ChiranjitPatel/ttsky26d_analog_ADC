### ss at 1.62 V

At ss with VDD = 1.62 V the 1 mV decision takes longer than this testbench's 50 ns evaluation window (10 MHz clock), so those three runs show no decision time and a distorted toggle point. `tb_cmp_ss.spice` repeats the worst case (ss, 125 °C, 1.62 V) with the ADC's 1 MHz clock (500 ns evaluation): all decisions at ±20 mV and ±2 mV are right and take 73–77 ns. A10 (≤ 50 ns) is missed at this corner only; the ADC at 1 MHz is unaffected, the maximum clock there is a few MHz (A12 asks for 10 MHz at tt only).

## Decision (2026-11-21)

Both misses are accepted for the tape-out (no redesign before the 11-26 freeze): the offset only shifts the transfer curve and is calibrated out (two-point offset/gain calibration in `docs/info.md`, test 2); the slow corner still converts correctly at the specified 1 MHz clock.
