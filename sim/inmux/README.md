# Stage 7 — inmux (ADC input multiplexer) + vcm_gen (common mode and top-plate switch)

Schematics: `xschem/inmux.sch`, `xschem/vcm_gen.sch` (generated by `xschem/gen_inmux.py`, `gen_vcm_gen.py`).
Drawing: `material/schematics/stage7_inmux_vcm.png`. Run: `./run.sh`.

## Design

- **inmux:** INSEL decoder (static NOR2/INV) and four transmission gates (nfet 4/0.15 + pfet_lvt 8/0.35)
  onto `vadc`, the DAC bottom-plate input bus. 00 → VIN (`ua[0]`), 01 → VPTAT, 10 → VBG, 11 → VGND.
- **vcm_gen:** 10 µA from `pbias` into a diode-connected nfet 4/1 gives vcm ≈ 0.716 V, decoupled by
  ≈ 2 pF of gate cap. Its value cancels (the top plate is sampled to vcm and compared against the same
  node), so only stability within a conversion matters. MTOP (nfet 1/0.15) holds the DAC top plate at
  vcm while sampling, and opens before the bottom plates switch (bottom-plate sampling).

## DAC switching scheme this assumes (fixed here for stages 8/9)

1. **Sample:** top plate = vcm (MTOP on), all bottom plates = `vadc`.
2. MTOP opens, *then* the bottom plates switch: MSB cap → VREF, all others → VGND, so
   top = vcm + VREF/2 − Vin, which stays inside ≈ 0.1 … 1.3 V (never below ground).
3. Each bit: comparator compares top vs vcm; q = 1 (top > vcm) means Vin < the trial level → that bit = 0,
   so its cap goes back to VGND; then the next cap goes to VREF.

## Results (tt, 27 °C, 1.8 V)

See `results.md`. Selection correct, DC crosstalk unmeasurable (< 1 µV), VIN sampled within 10 µV
after 1 µs (full-scale step through the pad model).

## Decision: VBG and VPTAT are converted with a slow clock

VPTAT (≈ 266 kΩ source) and VBG (≈ 240 kΩ) can't charge the 2.4 pF DAC within a 1 µs sample phase
(errors +134 mV / −188 mV after 1 µs). They settle to 0.5 LSB in 3.7 µs / 3.7 µs, and are exact
(< 1 µV) after 10 µs. **Rule: for INSEL = 01/10, run the ADC clock at ≤ 100 kHz** (10 µs sample
phase, ≥ 2.5× margin). The temperature sensor and bandgap check don't need speed, and this saves a
buffer (area and current). This goes in the test procedure (`docs/info.md`) and the RP2040 firmware.
